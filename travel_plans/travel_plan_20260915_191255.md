### 9. Final Tips

1. **Book Early** – Flights and JR Passes are cheaper 2–3 months ahead.  
2. **Stay Flexible** – Keep at least one day open for spontaneous detours.  
3. **Use a 7‑Day JR Pass** – Even if you don’t take Shinkansen every day, the pass covers all local trains and saves money.  
4. **Eat Local** – Convenience stores, izakayas, and street food are delicious and budget‑friendly.  
5. **Travel Light** – Avoid extra baggage fees on budget airlines.
